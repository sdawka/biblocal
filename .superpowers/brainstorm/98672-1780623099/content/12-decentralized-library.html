D

"See who is reading what near you."
A decentralized library, one shelf at a time. Find a new book and maybe a new friend.

My recommendation: Option A. The eyebrow is prime real estate for positioning — "A decentralized library for your neighborhood" immediately tells visitors what this is and makes it distinctive.
