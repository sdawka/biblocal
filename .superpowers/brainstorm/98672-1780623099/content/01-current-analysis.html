What's Working

  • Beautiful literary aesthetic that matches the audience
  • Evocative, personality-filled copy ("Om is my favorite tortoise philosopher")
  • Interactive demos (bookshelf hover, facet cards flip)
  • Clear visual hierarchy within each section

What Research Suggests Revisiting

  • 5-Second Clarity: "The right book finds you" is poetic but doesn't explain what biblocal is
  • CTA Placement: One CTA at the very end — research says repeat strategically (hero, mid-page, end)
  • Length: 8 sections before signup — users may drop off before understanding the value
  • Value Prop Buried: The "local book lending + taste matching" concept takes scrolling to discover
  • Benefit vs. Feature: Headlines describe features ("Books move") more than benefits ("Find your people")

The Core Tension

Your current page has literary personality that resonates with book lovers, but sacrifices immediate clarity about what the product does. The question is: how much clarity to add while preserving the vibe?
