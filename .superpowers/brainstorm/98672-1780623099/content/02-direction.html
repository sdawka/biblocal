What's the redesign priority?

This shapes how aggressively we restructure vs. refine

A

Clarity First

Restructure for conversion. Lead with a direct value prop ("Find book people near you"), consolidate to 4-5 sections, add CTAs throughout. Keep some literary touches but prioritize "what is this?" in the first 5 seconds.

Trade-off: May feel more "startup-y" and less distinctive
B

Personality + Clarity

Keep the literary voice but add a clear subhead under the poetic headline. Trim to 5-6 sections, add one mid-page CTA. The vibe stays, but visitors understand what this is faster.

Trade-off: Compromise — may not fully optimize either goal
C

Refine, Don't Restructure

Keep the 8-section flow but sharpen the copy. Make the hero subhead more concrete, tighten each section's headline, add one strategic CTA. The structure is intentional; just polish the words.

Trade-off: Won't address length/drop-off concerns

My recommendation: Option B. Your literary voice is a genuine differentiator for book lovers — most apps in this space feel generic. But the research is clear that immediate clarity matters. A hybrid approach lets you keep the personality while fixing the "what is this?" problem.
