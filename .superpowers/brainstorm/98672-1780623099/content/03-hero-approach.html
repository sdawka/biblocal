How should the hero communicate clarity?

The poetic headline stays — the question is what supports it

Current hero:

A place for book people to find each other
The right book finds you.
So do the right people.
Somewhere nearby, someone else finished that book at 2am and needed to talk about it. This is how you find them.
A

Concrete Eyebrow

Replace the eyebrow with something functional:

Local book lending & reader matching
The right book finds you. So do the right people.
Clarity up top, poetry below
B

Concrete Subhead

Keep the poetic eyebrow, make the subhead explain:

A place for book people to find each other
The right book finds you. So do the right people.
Build your shelf. Match with readers nearby. Lend, borrow, discuss.
Poetry leads, clarity follows
C

Both Layers

Functional eyebrow AND concrete subhead:

Local book lending & reader matching
The right book finds you. So do the right people.
Build your shelf. Match with readers nearby. Lend, borrow, discuss.
Maximum clarity, may feel over-explained

My recommendation: Option B. The current eyebrow ("A place for book people to find each other") already has warmth and sets the tone. Replacing the dreamy subhead with concrete actions ("Build your shelf. Match with readers nearby.") gives you clarity without losing the poetic headline.
