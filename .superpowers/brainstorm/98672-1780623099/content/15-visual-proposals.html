Proposed Visual & Motion Improvements

Based on the research and current implementation

1

Simplify Hero Animation

Currently 16 floating books with staggered delays up to 0.95s. With the shorter, punchier copy, simplify to fewer books (8-10) with faster entrance (max 0.5s delay). Less visual noise, faster perceived load.

Research: Pages that load in 1s convert 3x better than 5s
2

Add Hero Visual for Mobile

Floating books are hidden on mobile (display: none). Add a subtle alternative — perhaps 2-3 book covers flanking the headline, or a single animated book that "opens" on load.

Research: 63% of traffic is mobile; mobile converts 40-51% less
3

Improve Section Rhythm

With fewer sections, increase breathing room. Add slightly more padding between sections. Make the visual flow feel intentional rather than compressed.

4

Speed Up Facet Card Reveals

Currently cards auto-flip with 200ms stagger (total ~1.3s for all 5). Speed up to 150ms stagger so users see the content faster. The flip animation itself (0.8s) can stay.

5

Mid-CTA Entrance

The new mid-page CTA needs motion. Fade in after FacetShowcase cards finish revealing, with a subtle pulse or glow to draw attention without being aggressive.

6

Add Scroll Progress Indicator

Thin gold line at top of viewport showing how far through the page the user is. Subtle signal that there's more below, and satisfying completion feedback.
